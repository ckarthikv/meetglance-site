MeetGlance

Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

In short: MeetGlance has no server and no account. Your call is transcribed on your Mac and never recorded. Only the question, a short slice of the conversation and the relevant passages from your files go to the AI service you connect, to write the answer.

Your calls

  • While you're listening, call audio and your microphone are transcribed on your Mac (Apple's speech recognition on macOS 26, an on-device Whisper model on earlier versions).
  • Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded. The transcript lives in memory and is discarded when you stop listening.
  • MeetGlance only listens after you press Start listening. When a call starts it may show a nudge, but it never starts listening on its own.

Your files

  • Files and links you add are read and indexed on your Mac. MeetGlance remembers only which files you added, not their contents; they're re-read each launch.
  • For each answer, only the few passages needed are sent to your AI service, never whole files.

Your AI service

  • You connect your own Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter account. Your key is stored in your Mac's Keychain and sent only to that provider.
  • Each answer request contains the question, a short slice of the conversation and the relevant passages from your files. That provider's own privacy terms apply to what it receives.

Google (optional)

  • If you connect Google, MeetGlance asks for read-only access to your calendar events and access to only the Drive files you pick (the drive.file permission). It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Calendar events are used on your Mac to show your next meeting and add the files attached to it. Google sign-in tokens are kept in your Keychain. Nothing from Google is sent anywhere except, when you start listening, the relevant passages of the files you added, to your AI service as above.
  • MeetGlance's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect Google in the app at any time, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Updates

MeetGlance checks once a day for a new version by downloading a small update list from this website. That request includes only the app version, as any web request would.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No MeetGlance account, and nothing about you is stored by us.

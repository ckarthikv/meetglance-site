MeetGlance

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: your calls are transcribed on your Mac and never recorded. To write an answer, MeetGlance sends the question, a short slice of the conversation and a few passages from your files through our server to our AI provider. None of that is stored. Our server keeps only your Google account id, your email address and the minutes you've used.

This policy covers the MeetGlance app for Mac and this website. MeetGlance is made by Karthik Vaidyanath ("we", "us"). Contact: karthik.vaidyanath.89@gmail.com.

Your calls

  • MeetGlance only listens after you press Start listening. It may nudge you when a call starts, but it never starts listening on its own.
  • While listening, call audio and your microphone are transcribed on your Mac, using Apple's speech recognition on macOS 26 or an on-device Whisper model on earlier versions.
  • Audio is never recorded, saved or uploaded. The transcript is kept in memory and discarded when you stop listening.

Your files

  • Files and links you add are read and indexed on your Mac. The app remembers which files you added, not their contents.
  • For each answer, only the few passages needed are sent, never whole files.

Answers and the AI provider

  • When a question is heard, the app sends the question, a short slice of the recent conversation and the relevant passages from your files to the MeetGlance server, which passes them to Anthropic (Claude) to write the answer.
  • The server does not store or log this content. It keeps only technical timings (how long a request took), with no content attached.
  • Anthropic processes the request under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on this data. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • If you haven't added any files, nothing from your call is sent.

Your account

  • You sign in with Google. Our server stores your Google account id, your email address, the minutes you've used, your subscription status and when your account was created. Minutes are counted while you're listening, one at a time.
  • We use this only to run your account: free minutes, your subscription, and limits that prevent abuse.
  • When subscriptions open, payments will be handled by our payment provider. We will never see your card details.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing karthik.vaidyanath.89@gmail.com. We delete it within 30 days.

Google user data

MeetGlance asks for each Google permission separately, only when you use the feature that needs it.

  • Sign in (openid, email): your email address and Google account id, used to create and identify your MeetGlance account.
  • Google Drive (drive.file, optional): access to only the Drive files you pick or that MeetGlance opens for you. It cannot see the rest of your Drive. File contents are read and indexed on your Mac, and only the passages needed for an answer are sent as described above.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events.readonly, optional): read-only access to your calendar events. These are used on your Mac to show your next meeting and to add the files attached to it. Calendar data is not sent to our server.
  • Google sign-in tokens are kept in your Mac's Keychain. Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. People do not read it.
  • MeetGlance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect Drive or Calendar in the app at any time, and revoke all access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Updates

The app checks once a day for a new version by downloading a small update list from this website. That request includes only the app version, as any web request would.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising, in the app or on this website.
  • We never sell or share your data, except with the providers named above to run the service.

Children

MeetGlance is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.

Contact

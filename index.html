Straight answers

Is this cheating?+

No — it’s built for your own meetings: your deck, your documents, your call. MeetGlance surfaces your own material and you do the talking. It isn’t made for interviews, exams, or anyone else’s assessment of you.

Can anyone on the call see the panel?+

The panel is excluded from screen-share capture, and a built-in Verify test lets you confirm that before a call. Honestly, though: no app can guarantee exclusion on every macOS version and every sharing mode — sharing a single window rather than your whole screen is safest.

Which Macs and macOS versions does it need?+

macOS 14.4 (Sonoma) or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. It’s designed for Macs with a camera notch; on other Macs it sits at the top centre of the screen. On macOS 26 it uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition; on 14.4–15 it downloads an on-device speech model (about 500 MB) once. The built-in Verify test confirms screen-share exclusion before you rely on it.

What does it cost?+

Every account starts with 100 free minutes of listening, with no card needed. After that it’s $20 a month for unlimited calls. The AI is included, so there’s no API key to set up. Minutes only count while MeetGlance is listening.

What does MeetGlance do with my Google account?+

Signing in shares only your email address. If you choose to, you can also let MeetGlance open the Drive files you pick (it can’t see the rest of your Drive) and read your calendar events, to add the files attached to your next meeting. Each permission is asked for separately, the first time you use it. See the privacy policy for details.

FAQ

Does MeetGlance record my calls?

No. Audio is transcribed on your Mac and never saved or uploaded. The transcript is kept in memory and discarded when you stop listening. Only the question and the few passages needed for an answer are sent out, and they aren’t stored.

Can anyone on the call see it?

The panel is excluded from screen sharing, and there’s no bot in the meeting. A built-in check confirms the panel is hidden before you rely on it. No app can guarantee this on every macOS version and sharing mode, so sharing a single window is safest.

Is this cheating?

MeetGlance is built for your own meetings, with your own material: your deck, your numbers, your call. You do the talking. It isn’t made for interviews, exams or any other assessment of you.

Which Macs does it work on?

macOS 14.4 (Sonoma) or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. It’s designed for Macs with a camera notch; on other Macs it sits at the top centre of the screen. On macOS 14 and 15 it downloads an on-device speech model (about 500 MB) once.

What does it cost?

Every account starts with 100 free minutes of listening, no card needed. After that it’s $20 a month for unlimited calls, and you can cancel anytime. The AI is included.

What does MeetGlance do with my Google account?

Signing in shares only your email address. If you choose to, you can let MeetGlance open the Drive files you pick and read your calendar events to add the files attached to your next meeting. Each permission is asked for separately. See the privacy policy.
